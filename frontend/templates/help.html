Frequently Asked Questions

How do I translate a video?

  1. Open the Upload Video page.
  2. Select your video file.
  3. Choose the target language.
  4. Click Translate.
  5. Download the translated video when processing completes.

Which video formats are supported?

MP4, AVI, MOV and MKV.

Does Bhasha Mitra work offline?

Yes. Bhasha Mitra is designed to run completely offline once all required AI models are installed.

Where are translated files stored?

Completed translations are available in the My Translations page.
