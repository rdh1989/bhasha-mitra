About Bhasha Mitra

Breaking language barriers through secure, offline AI-powered video translation.

Our Mission

Bhasha Mitra is an AI-powered offline video translation platform designed to make multilingual communication simple, secure, and accessible.

The application automatically extracts speech from videos, converts it into text, translates it into the selected language, and generates translated subtitles and audio while keeping all processing completely offline.

Key Features

  • 🎥 AI-powered video translation
  • 🎙 Speech-to-Text (ASR)
  • 🌐 Multi-language translation
  • 📝 Subtitle generation
  • 🔊 AI voice synthesis (future enhancement)
  • 🔒 100% Offline Processing
  • ⚡ FastAPI-powered backend
  • 💻 Modern web-based dashboard
