Help & Support

Quick answers to common questions.

Getting started

  1. Choose video files stored on this computer.
  2. Choose the source language and the target dubbing language.
  3. Click Start dubbing. Each selected video becomes its own job.
  4. Track progress in Recent Jobs, or check Job History any time.
  5. Once a job is Completed, click Download to get the dubbed video.

Frequently asked questions

Why is processing so slow?

Everything runs on your computer's CPU (no cloud, no GPU required). Speech recognition and speech synthesis are the most CPU-intensive steps; a short video can still take several minutes. Larger videos take proportionally longer.

Why did my job fail with a "language not supported" error?

Some low-resource languages (e.g. Odia, Bodo, Konkani, Maithili, Manipuri, Santali, Dogri) can only be used as a dubbing target, not as source audio, because the speech recognition model was never trained on them. See the About page for the full list.

Can I submit multiple videos at once?

Yes. Enter multiple paths (one per line) in the box on the Dashboard - each becomes its own job and up to several run in parallel automatically.

I don't see the "Start dubbing" form or "User Administration" link.

Job submission requires the operator or admin role, and user administration requires the admin role. The user role is read-only by design. Ask an administrator to adjust your role if needed.

I forgot my password.

Ask an administrator to reset it from the User Administration page.

Where are my files stored?

Videos are never uploaded or copied - the app reads them directly from the local path you provide. Generated audio and dubbed outputs are stored under this application's outputs/<video-name>/<job-id>/ folder. Nothing is uploaded to the internet, except a one-time, small tokenizer download the first time the speech synthesis model runs.

Still stuck?

Check the application log file (logs/app.log) for detailed error messages, or contact your system administrator.
