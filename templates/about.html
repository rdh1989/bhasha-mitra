About Bhasha Mitra

What this application does and how it works under the hood.

What it does

Bhasha Mitra takes a video, transcribes its speech, translates the text into a target language, synthesizes natural-sounding speech in that language, and re-combines it with the original video to produce a dubbed video - entirely on your local machine's CPU, with no data ever leaving your computer.

The pipeline

  1. Speech recognition - faster-whisper (CTranslate2) transcribes the source audio and auto-detects its language.
  2. Translation - IndicTrans2 (English → Indic) converts the transcript to the target language.
  3. Speech synthesis - Indic Parler-TTS generates natural speech in the target language for each sentence.
  4. Re-assembly - the dubbed speech is time-aligned to the original segments and muxed back into the video.

Translation direction & language support

The bundled IndicTrans2 checkpoint only translates from English into Indic languages. To still support routes like Marathi → Hindi or Marathi → English, Bhasha Mitra uses the speech model's own built-in speech-to-English translation as a pivot:

  • Source language = target language → no translation needed.
  • Source is English → translated directly into the target Indic language.
  • Target is English → the speech model translates the source speech to English directly.
  • Neither is English → the speech model translates to English first, then IndicTrans2 converts English → target.

A handful of very low-resource languages (Bodo, Dogri, Konkani, Maithili, Manipuri, Santali, Odia) are not in the speech model's training data, so they cannot be used as source audio, only as dubbing targets.
